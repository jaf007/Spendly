Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as when you log in, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate Spendly's core features: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and reports of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your information will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. We may use trusted third-party providers for essential infrastructure, such as hosting and email delivery, and these providers only access the minimum data necessary to perform their function on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page, and we will respond as soon as possible.
